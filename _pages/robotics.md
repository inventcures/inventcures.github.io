    <h3><a href="https://x.com/drmapavone/status/2113282303564956047" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Learn robot autonomy at Stanford, now open to everyone</a></h3>
    <p class="pd-meta">Marco Pavone (<a href="https://x.com/drmapavone" target="_blank" rel="noopener">@drmapavone</a>) · X · <time datetime="2026-10-22">22 Oct 2026</time></p>

    <h3><a href="https://ekarobotics.com/blog/eka-robotics-raises-150m-to-build-superhuman-intelligence-for-the-physical-world" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Eka Robotics Raises $150M to Build Superhuman Intelligence for the Physical World</a></h3>
    <p class="pd-meta">Eka Robotics · Company blog · <time datetime="2026-10-20">20 Oct 2026</time></p>
    <p class="pd-summary">Pulkit Agrawal (MIT) and Tuomas Haarnoja (ex-DeepMind) raise a $150M Series A, led by a16z and F-Prime at a $1.45B valuation, for dexterous manipulation that is both general and fast. Instead of imitating human demos, their vision-force-action (VFA) model adds touch and force to sight and is trained by RL in simulation, AlphaZero-style; they claim about 1,500 picks an hour, around 2.5x a human.</p>

  <li class="pd-item">
    <h3><a href="https://www.construction-physics.com/p/how-to-train-your-robot" target="_blank" rel="noopener">How to Train Your Robot</a></h3>
    <p class="pd-meta">Brian Potter · Construction Physics · <time datetime="2026-10-15">15 Oct 2026</time></p>
    <p class="pd-summary">Robotics has no internet-scale corpus, so Potter surveys how robot training data gets made: teleoperation, real-world RL, simulation, synthetic data, and human video or handheld UMI rigs. Today's robot datasets look roughly 1,000x smaller than LLM text corpora, and closing that gap could cost $10–100B, unless better architectures need less data.</p>
